# AuthMaster chapter standard

Every topic is a directory with `README.md`, `examples.js`, `exercises.md`, and `solutions.md`.

A complete chapter should include: precise definition; simple explanation; motivation; mental model; prerequisites; terminology; flow diagram; internals; rules and edge cases; correct and incorrect examples; runnable code; real-world usage; browser/Node differences where relevant; security implications; debugging; alternatives and trade-offs; beginner/intermediate/advanced exercises; solutions; teach-back; and mastery checklist.

Depth must be meaningful, not filler. Split a large subject into smaller folders instead of compressing it into a shallow overview. Clearly label pseudocode. Never imply a teaching example is production-safe. Prefer official standards and maintained library documentation.
